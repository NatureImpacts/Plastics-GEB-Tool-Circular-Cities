What this tool is. It reproduces the baseline calculation from the Circular Cities workshop workbook. The baseline is what your city emits to the ocean if nothing changes. The global environmental benefit is the reduction you achieve against it, which is what the lever panel estimates.

Where the prefilled numbers come from. Population, total waste, plastic waste, collection coverage and growth rates are from the project dataset for the participating cities. The two leakage percentages are deliberately left empty: no dataset can set them for your city.

Calculation steps
  1. Collected plastic = plastic × collection rate; uncollected is the remainder.
  2. Marine plastic from the uncollected stream = uncollected × emissions% × marine%.
  3. Marine plastic from the collected stream = collected × emissions% × marine%.
  4. Annual total is the sum of the two streams.
  5. Project total compounds the annual figure at the growth rate: annual × ((1+g)^n − 1) / g, or annual × n when growth is zero.
