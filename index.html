What this tool is. It reproduces the baseline calculation from the Circular Cities workshop workbook. The baseline is what your city emits to the ocean each year if nothing changes.

Where the prefilled numbers come from. Population, total waste, plastic waste and collection coverage are from the project dataset for the participating cities. Annual population growth is fixed, taken from World Bank data. The two leakage percentages are deliberately left empty: no dataset can set them for your city.

Calculation steps
  1. Collected plastic = plastic × collection rate; uncollected is the remainder.
  2. Marine plastic from the uncollected stream = uncollected × emissions% × marine%.
  3. Marine plastic from the collected stream = collected × emissions% × marine%.
  4. Annual total is the sum of the two streams.
  5. Project total compounds the annual figure at the growth rate: annual × ((1+g)^n − 1) / g, or annual × n when growth is zero.
